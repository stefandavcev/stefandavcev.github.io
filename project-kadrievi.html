Website case study

Kadrievi - music, events and media in one WordPress experience.

A content-rich website for a Belgium-based orchestra, bringing the group’s story, events, video performances, photo galleries and booking information into a single clear destination.

Visit live siteAll work
ClientKadrievi
ScopeWebsite, content & visual direction
ToolsWordPress, Elementor, Photoshop, HTML/CSS
My roleLead web design, information architecture, copy editing & implementation

The challenge

Kadrievi had a rich mix of events, performances, photography, video and booking information. The main challenge was to make that volume of content feel like one coherent experience—not a collection of disconnected pages.

My approach

I defined the page structure, designed the visual system, shaped and edited the website copy, and implemented the responsive experience in WordPress. The hierarchy follows the visitor’s natural journey: understand the orchestra, experience the performances, find an event and make contact. A dark, high-contrast direction gives the media room to feel expressive without weakening usability.

What I delivered

  • Information architecture for events, videos, galleries and booking
  • Website design and reusable visual components
  • Website copy editing and content hierarchy
  • Responsive WordPress and Elementor implementation
  • Image preparation and visual QA across screen sizes
A flexible content system keeps the site useful for the audience and practical for the client to update.
